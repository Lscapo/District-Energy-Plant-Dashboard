Upload BAS trends

Choose one or more BAS trend files (CSV). Tell the dashboard which column is which (for Plant Uptime: supply temperature and its setpoint, differential pressure and its setpoint), then Calculate to preview Plant Uptime and Plant Efficiency. Only the small daily results are saved, never the raw trends. Shorter BAS pulls are logged more often (for example 5 minutes for a day or a week, 15 for two weeks, 30 for a month, hourly for two months, daily for a year), and the more often, the more accurate the scores. Trends logged less often than the limit in Settings (60 minutes to start) are not scored. Exchanger setpoints you cannot trend are taken from the plant rounds. Upload whole days: a day uploaded again is replaced, unless it is already stored with more data.
